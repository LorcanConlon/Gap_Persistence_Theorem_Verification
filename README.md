
## Verification of gap persistence counterexamples

The following Jupyter notebooks provide explicit constructions
and computational verification of counterexamples discussed in
the accompanying manuscript.

### 1. SLDCRB–most-informative gap persistence

[View Jupyter notebook](01_sld_mi_counterexample.ipynb)

Three-parameter, rank-deficient counterexample demonstrating
that the gap between the SLD Cramér–Rao bound and the most
informative bound can close after two copies.

The notebook includes an exact rational separability
certificate consisting of ten positive-definite matrices.

### 2. Two-parameter counterexample (Appendix D.2)

[View Jupyter notebook](02_appendix_D2.ipynb)

Explicit two-parameter counterexample demonstrating
two-copy attainability despite a strict single-copy gap.

The notebook constructs the attaining two-copy projective
measurement and verifies its local unbiasedness and cost.

## Requirements

Python 3 with NumPy and SymPy.

Open either notebook in GitHub for inspection or download
it to execute the calculations in JupyterLab.
